OpenDyslexic by Abbie Gonzalez.
Font files accessed at runtime from the archived author repository via jsDelivr.
SIL Open Font License 1.1: https://github.com/antijingoist/opendyslexic/blob/main/OFL.txt
The font is used optionally in NovelWeaver and does not alter manuscript contents.
For reliable offline fonts in an installed PWA, open the updated site while online at least once and choose OpenDyslexic before switching offline.
